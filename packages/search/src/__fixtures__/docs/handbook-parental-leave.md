# Parental Leave

Northwind Systems offers paid parental leave to every employee who has been on
payroll for at least ninety consecutive days. Parental leave covers birth,
adoption, and long-term foster placement. There is no distinction between
primary and secondary caregivers: the entitlement is the same for everyone.

## Entitlement

A birthing parent receives twenty weeks of fully paid parental leave. A
non-birthing parent receives sixteen weeks of fully paid parental leave. Both
entitlements are per child, not per year, so a parent welcoming twins receives
one entitlement rather than two. An employee who adopts a child older than
twelve receives twelve weeks of paid parental leave.

Parental leave is paid at one hundred percent of base salary. Commission,
on-call allowances, and the annual bonus are calculated as if the employee had
worked their normal schedule for the whole of the leave period. Equity vesting
continues on its ordinary schedule; parental leave never pauses a vesting
cliff.

## Taking the leave

Leave may be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first
twenty-four months after the birth or placement. Each block must be at least
two consecutive weeks. A parent who wants to return gradually may instead take
the final four weeks of their entitlement as half days over eight weeks, with
manager agreement.

Notify your manager and the People team at least thirty days before the leave
starts, or as soon as is practical if the birth or placement happens early. The
People team files the request in the HR system and confirms the payroll dates
in writing. No medical documentation is required beyond the standard proof of
birth or placement.

## While you are away

Your laptop, accounts, and building access stay active for the duration of the
leave. You are not expected to read email, attend meetings, or respond to
messages. Your manager appoints a cover owner for each of your active projects
before the leave starts and records the handover in the team's project tracker.

Company holidays that fall inside the leave period do not extend the leave.
Accrued annual leave continues to accrue at the normal rate and may be taken
immediately after the parental leave ends.

## Returning to work

A returning parent keeps the same role, level, and compensation band they held
before the leave. Any company-wide compensation review that happened during
the leave applies retroactively from the review's effective date.

For the first eight weeks after returning, an employee may work a reduced
schedule of four days per week at full pay. Parents who need dedicated space
for expressing milk should contact the office team, who will reserve a private
room for the required hours on the days the employee is on site.

If an employee decides not to return, the company does not claw back parental
leave pay. We ask only that the decision is communicated before the last week
of the leave so that the cover arrangement can be made permanent.

## Related policies

Bereavement leave, carer's leave, and fertility treatment leave are described
in their own sections of the handbook. Parental leave sits on top of these and
never reduces them.
