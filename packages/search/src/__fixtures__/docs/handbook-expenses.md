# Expenses and Travel

This policy covers how Northwind Systems employees spend company money and how
they get it back. The guiding rule is simple: spend it as if it were your own,
and be able to explain the receipt to a colleague without embarrassment.

## Getting reimbursed

Submit every expense in the expense portal within thirty days of the purchase.
Attach a legible receipt, choose the matching category, and write one sentence
of business purpose. "Client dinner, Acme renewal discussion" is enough;
"dinner" is not. Claims without a receipt are capped at twenty-five pounds and
need manager approval in the comment thread.

Approved claims are reimbursed in the next payroll run. Payroll closes on the
eighteenth of each month, so a claim approved on the nineteenth is paid the
following month. Reimbursement is always made to the bank account on file for
salary; we do not reimburse to personal cards or payment apps.

A claim rejected by a manager returns to the submitter with a reason. Resubmit
with the missing information rather than opening a new claim, so the audit
trail stays on one record.

## Flights and trains

Book all travel through the company travel desk. Flights under six hours are
economy class. Flights over six hours may be booked in premium economy, and
overnight flights of more than nine hours may be booked in business class with
director approval recorded in the booking.

Rail travel within the country is standard class, except where a first class
advance fare is cheaper than the standard walk-up fare, which is common and
entirely acceptable. Book at least fourteen days ahead where the trip is known;
last-minute fare differences are the single largest avoidable cost in this
policy.

Airport lounge access, seat selection fees, and checked baggage for trips of
three nights or more are all reimbursable. Airline loyalty points earned on
company travel belong to the traveller.

## Hotels and meals

Hotel rates are capped at two hundred pounds per night in London, New York, and
Zurich, and one hundred and forty pounds per night everywhere else. The cap
includes local taxes. If no room is available under the cap, book the cheapest
available room and note the search in the claim.

Meals on a travel day are reimbursed on actuals up to sixty pounds per day,
including non-alcoholic drinks. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a
client entertainment claim, never as part of a solo travel meal. Room service
counts toward the daily meal cap.

## Equipment and software

Employees may spend up to five hundred pounds per year on home office equipment
without pre-approval: chairs, desks, monitors, keyboards, and headsets. Laptops
and phones are issued centrally by the IT team and are never a personal expense
claim.

Software subscriptions are never an expense claim. Request them through the
procurement queue so the contract, the security review, and the renewal date
live in one place.

## Mileage and local transport

Driving a personal car on company business is reimbursed at forty-five pence
per mile for the first ten thousand miles in a tax year and twenty-five pence
per mile after that. Log the start point, end point, and purpose. Parking and
tolls are reimbursed on receipt. Traffic fines and speeding penalties are
never reimbursed.

Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursable when public transport is unavailable,
when carrying equipment, or when travelling after twenty-two hundred hours.
